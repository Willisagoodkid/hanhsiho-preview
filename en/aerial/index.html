Reality Capture

Bring the site into the model, from above

We combine drone photogrammetry, ground capture and laser scanning to turn real environments into measurable, comparable data that feeds straight into BIM and GIS — so design, construction and operations start from the same ground truth.

01 · Reality Mesh

3D Reality Models

Aerial and ground imagery are rebuilt into textured 3D models of existing conditions, ready for design review, urban digitisation and heritage documentation.

02 · GIS

3D / 2D GIS Integration

Reality models, terrain and BIM share one geographic reference, so buildings line up with their surroundings, utilities and cadastral data. Query, analyse and plan on a single map.

03 · Laser Scanning

Laser Point-Cloud Scanning

Laser scanning captures dense 3D point clouds of existing structures, terrain and earthworks — the basis for as-built models, construction deviation checks and complete digital records of historic buildings.
